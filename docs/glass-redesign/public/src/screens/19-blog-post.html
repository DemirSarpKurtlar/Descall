The short answer

Pick Descall when your group wants a lighter Discord alternative for chat, voice, screen share, and LFG. Keep Discord when you still run large public communities and bots.

Chat & presence

Both apps deliver real-time messaging. Descall focuses on DMs, groups, and clear presence — less server administration for friend circles.

Descall uses modern WebRTC with TURN support and screen-share quality presets. Discord remains battle-tested at massive scale; Descall optimizes for squad-sized calls.

Descall keeps communication free and sells cosmetics. Discord’s Nitro unlocks many quality-of-life extras. If Nitro fatigue is why you searched “Discord alternative,” Descall is the direct answer.
