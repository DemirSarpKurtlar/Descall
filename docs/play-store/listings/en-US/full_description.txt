Descall is a chat and calling app for talking with your friends, your squad and your communities. From one-on-one messages to busy group calls and servers, all your conversations live in one place.

MESSAGING
• Private one-on-one messages and group chats with friends
• Share photos, videos, files, GIFs and emoji
• Record and send voice messages
• React with emoji, reply to, edit and pin messages
• Search messages within a chat
• Push notifications for new messages and calls

VOICE AND VIDEO CALLS
• One-on-one voice and video calls with friends
• Start a group voice or video call with your whole crew in a group chat with one tap
• Incoming calls arrive as a ringing notification; tap it and Descall opens and connects you to the call
• Mute your mic, turn your camera on and off, and see who is talking
• Recent calls and quick dial in the Calls tab; call cards in chats show duration and participants

SERVERS AND COMMUNITIES
• Create your own server with text channels and voice rooms
• Organize channels into categories and manage permissions with roles
• Join voice rooms with a single tap, see who is talking and turn on your camera if you like
• Kick, ban, timeout and message-deletion tools for server owners and moderators
• Discover public servers or join with an invite link

FRIENDS AND PROFILE
• Add friends and see who is online and what they are up to
• Make your profile yours with a photo, banner, bio and status

SAFETY AND CONTROL
• Sign in with a username and password
• Block users: people you block can't message you, call you or send you friend requests
• Report objectionable messages and users from inside the app
• Profanity in messages is automatically masked with ***
• Calls are never recorded
• Two-step verification, plus a list of active sessions you can sign out remotely
• Delete your account at any time from Settings

Descall is intended for users aged 13 and over.
