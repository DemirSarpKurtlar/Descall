TESTING CALLS
- One-on-one: Chats (top icon on the left rail) > open the chat > phone or camera icon (top right). Incoming one-on-one calls ring through CallKit, also in the background or when locked.
- Group: Groups (second icon) > create a group with the other account > phone or camera icon. Group calls arrive as a notification/in-app prompt, not CallKit.
- Locked phone: the calling engine runs in the app's WKWebView, which can't use the mic while locked. Accept on the call screen; it then shows "Open Descall to connect". Unlock and open Descall and the accepted call joins automatically (it ends cleanly after 60 seconds if the app isn't opened). This is intentional and explained on screen.
- Servers/voice rooms: "+" (Add New) on the left rail > create a server. It comes with a #general text channel and a "General" voice room; tap it > "Join Voice". Invite account B via the server menu > "Invite People".
Calls are never recorded; audio/video is only relayed in real time.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Settings (gear, bottom of the left rail) > Security > "Delete account". Confirm with the password (Apple accounts type their username). The user is signed out everywhere at once, the Apple token is revoked and data is permanently deleted after a 14-day grace period (signing in again cancels it), as the app explains before confirming.

SAFETY (Guideline 1.2)
- Terms with zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users must be accepted before an account is created.
- Report: tap a message from another user > flag icon, or open a profile > "Report". Reports reach our moderation queue instantly.
- Block: profile > "Block" (also in the DM header and member/DM menus). Blocked users can't message, call or friend-request you, you get no notifications from them and their messages are collapsed. Unblock in Settings > Security > Blocked users.
- Profanity in messages, replies, previews, pinned messages and notifications is masked with *** in the iOS app.
- Server owners/moderators can kick, ban, time out and delete messages. The developer reviews every report within 24 hours and removes content or suspends/bans accounts.
- Users must be 13+ (date of birth required).

VALORANT LFG: crosshair icon on the left rail > browse or create lobbies by mode, region and rank range. Players pick their rank themselves; no Riot sign-in in the iOS app. Not affiliated with Riot Games.

NOT IN THE iOS APP: screen sharing, casino mini-games and Riot account linking.
